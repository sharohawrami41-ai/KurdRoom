KurdRoom — NEW: Workspace hub (premium, per-department AI tools)
================================================================

BIG PICTURE
A new top-menu section "🚀 Workspace" (gold). Students pick their FIELD and get
AI tools built for that major's assignments. Everything in Workspace is Premium.
All the older tools are now FREE.

WHAT'S IN THE WORKSPACE (10 fields, ~21 tools)
  Architecture & Design : Sketch → Render, AI Design Studio
  Civil Engineering     : Structural Calculator, Bill of Quantities, Lab/Site Report
  Computer Science & IT : Coding Helper, Diagram Generator, Project Planner
  Medicine & Health     : Flashcards + MCQs, Clinical Case Tutor, High-Yield Notes
  Business & Accounting : Business Plan, Finance & Accounting, Case-Study Solver
  Graphic Design & Media: Brand & Poster Concepts, Sketch → Render
  Law                   : Case Brief, Legal Memo (IRAC), Concept Explainer
  Sciences & Math       : Step-by-Step Solver, Lab Report Writer
  Education & Teaching  : Lesson Plan Maker, Worksheet Maker
  Languages & Translation: Writing Polish, Academic Translator, IELTS/TOEFL Coach
Each tool streams live, has the reply box, and accepts a PDF upload.

FIRST-TIME TOUR
The very first time a user opens the Workspace, an auto-playing guided tour explains
what it is and previews the services (5 slides, Skip/Next). Shown only once per device.
